Frequently Asked Questions

How do I redeem a game?

To redeem a game, log in to your account, navigate to 'My Library', and enter the redemption code provided at purchase. The game will be added to your library instantly.

How can I request a refund?

You can request a refund within 14 days of purchase, provided the game has been played for less than 2 hours. Go to 'Orders', select the game, and click 'Request Refund'.

What payment methods are supported?

We support all major credit/debit cards, PayPal, and digital wallets. All transactions are encrypted and secure.

Can I gift a game to a friend?

Yes! During checkout, select 'Gift Purchase' and enter your friend's email. They will receive a code to redeem the game.

How do I track my order?

After purchase, go to 'Orders' in your account dashboard. Each order shows its status and download availability.
